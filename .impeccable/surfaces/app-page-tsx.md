# CoBusiness landing page

Scope: public, responsive landing page at `/`. Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience: Bolivian importers and commercial operators first, with small business buyers as participants. The visitor needs to understand how separate purchasing commitments can combine toward an operator's MOQ, what the MVP demonstrates, and what is planned later. Primary action: “Recorrer el MVP”, which moves to the MVP and roadmap sections; there is no signup or contact flow in this repository.

Content and constraints: Spanish copy. Phase 1 is an MVP planned for Stellar Testnet with demo assets and no real customer funds. Show operator-created offers, buyer contributions, an MOQ and deadline, the simulated commercial approval required for a successful release, and eligible refunds if the target is missed. Later phases are roadmap scope, not implemented product capabilities; show no delivery dates. Do not invent customers, transaction evidence, company names, performance metrics, savings, adoption, or operational outcomes. Visually mark the order panel and workflow as illustrative.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Make the collective order itself the clearest explanation of how independent buyer demand can reach an operator's minimum order quantity.

OWN-WORLD: Use the category-standard B2B landing vocabulary chosen by the user: open white surfaces, near-black text, restrained blue actions, a precise product grid, and compact neutral borders. Keep the visual language clean, readable, and conventional.

STORY: Importers recognize a structured offer and its commercial conditions. Buyers see how their demand joins one order. Both roles understand the Testnet-only MVP and the conditional success and refund paths, then can inspect the undated four-phase roadmap.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A slim wordmark and three anchor links sit above a two-column hero. The left column carries the headline, short B2B explanation, primary “Recorrer el MVP” action, and Testnet/no-real-funds note. The right column holds one large, explicitly illustrative collective-order panel with participant roles, combined demand, MOQ, deadline, conditional release, and eligible-refund paths. The MVP roadmap begins directly below.
